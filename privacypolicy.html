Privacy Policy

Application: ClipAlbum (the "App")

Effective Date: June 7, 2026 | Last Updated: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. It applies worldwide wherever the App is made available and is intended to comply with applicable privacy and data-protection laws, including those in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union / European Economic Area, Switzerland, Australia, New Zealand, Brazil, Japan, South Korea, Mainland China, and other regions. Where the mandatory laws of your country or region grant you greater rights, those laws prevail.

In one sentence: The App is a local-first private album. Your photos, videos, and album data are, by default, stored only on your own device. We operate no servers and do not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal information.

1. Who We Are

The App is provided by Maoxia Xiang ("we", "us", or "our"). For privacy questions, contact us using the details in Section 13. Where applicable law requires a "data controller" or equivalent, we act in that capacity solely with respect to the limited matters described below.

2. Our Core Privacy Principle

The App is designed to work without collecting your personal information. We do not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive your content or usage data.

3. Information We Process

3.1 Local Storage (Default)

Photos and videos you import are stored at original quality inside the App's private sandbox on your device. The App also generates thumbnails locally and stores the folders, tags, custom names, pinned status, and sort order you create in a local database. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is never shared with third parties.

3.2 Photo Library Access

To import media, the App requests permission to access your system photo library only when you actively choose to import. This permission is used solely to read the items you select; processing occurs locally on your device, and we do not upload library content to our servers. If you enable "Delete original from system photo library after import," the App deletes the corresponding original locally on your device after a successful import.

3.3 iCloud Sync (Optional, Pro Feature)

If you enable iCloud Sync, the App uses Apple's CloudKit private database to sync album structure and metadata (folders, tags, names, sort order), and iCloud Drive to sync original media files and thumbnails across your own devices. This data is stored in your iCloud account, governed by the Apple Privacy Policy, and we have no access to it. You can disable sync at any time; switching sync requires restarting the App to complete data migration.

3.4 In-App Purchases

Pro is unlocked through Apple App Store In-App Purchase. All payments are processed by Apple; we do not collect or receive your payment details. We only read Apple-provided purchase records to determine your Pro entitlement.

3.5 What We Do Not Collect

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, or device identifiers for tracking, and we do not profile you or use your data for advertising.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Because we do not collect or process personal data on our own servers, we generally do not act as a data controller for ongoing processing on backend systems. To the extent any on-device processing occurs (for example, photo-library access or iCloud sync that you initiate), the legal basis depends on your jurisdiction:

  • UK / EU / EEA / Switzerland: your consent and/or performance of our agreement with you under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or Swiss FADP, as applicable; you may withdraw consent by discontinuing the relevant feature or permission.
  • Mainland China: processing necessary to provide features you request, based on your consent under the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), which you may withdraw by disabling the feature or permission.
  • Brazil, Japan, South Korea, and other regions: similar bases of consent and/or contract performance under applicable local law, where processing is limited to on-device or Apple-managed services as described in Section 3.

5. Disclosure of Information

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only if required to do so by law, regulation, or valid legal process — however, because your content remains on your device, we generally have nothing to disclose.

6. No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell and do not share your personal information, including as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar U.S. state laws. We do not engage in "targeted advertising" or process your data for such purposes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: Uninstalling the App removes all photos, videos, thumbnails, and the album database stored in its sandbox. This is irreversible — back up first.
  • In-app deletion: You can delete individual files or folders at any time within the App.
  • iCloud data: If you used iCloud Sync, manage or delete the data via Settings and your system iCloud settings.

8. Security

Your files are stored in the App's private directory protected by the operating system. We recommend enabling a device passcode and Face ID / Touch ID. No method of storage is completely secure.

9. International Data Transfers

We do not transfer your personal information internationally because we do not collect it. Any iCloud data is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's policies.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (United States, in compliance with COPPA), under 16 (where applicable in the UK/EEA), or under the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction. Because we collect no personal information, no such data is gathered from children.

You always have full local control of your data within the App. In addition, depending on where you live, you may have the following statutory rights. To exercise any right, contact us (Section 13).

  • United States – California (CCPA/CPRA): the rights to know, access, delete, and correct personal information, to opt out of sale/sharing (we do none), and to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.
  • Other U.S. States (e.g., Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas): comparable rights of access, deletion, correction, and opt-out, where applicable.
  • United Kingdom / EEA (UK GDPR / GDPR): the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. You may lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) or your local supervisory authority.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): the rights to access your personal information and to challenge our compliance. You may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC).
  • Australia (Privacy Act 1988 & Australian Privacy Principles): the rights to access and correct personal information. You may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
  • New Zealand (Privacy Act 2020): the rights to access and correct personal information. You may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (NZ).
  • European Union / European Economic Area (EU GDPR): the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority in the EU/EEA member state where you live or work.
  • Switzerland (FADP): the rights of access, rectification, and erasure, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
  • Brazil (LGPD): the rights of confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, blocking, deletion, portability, and information about sharing (we do not sell or share your data).
  • Japan (APPI): the right to request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, and erasure of retained personal information, subject to applicable law.
  • South Korea (PIPA): the rights of access, correction, deletion, and suspension of processing of personal information.
  • Mainland China (PIPL): the rights to access, copy, correct, delete, and withdraw consent regarding personal information processed for App features you use; processing occurs on your device or in your iCloud account as described in Section 3, and we do not receive your media on our servers.
  • India (DPDP Act 2023): where applicable, the rights to access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal.
  • Other regions: any additional rights granted by mandatory local privacy or consumer laws.

We will respond to verifiable requests within the timeframe required by applicable law.
